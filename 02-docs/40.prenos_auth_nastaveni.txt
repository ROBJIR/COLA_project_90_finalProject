Django Auth settings
========================

01 / export
--------------
python3 manage.py dumpdata auth.group --natural-foreign --natural-primary --indent 2 --output groups.json
python3 manage.py dumpdata auth.group auth.user --natural-foreign --natural-primary --indent 2 --output accounts.json
